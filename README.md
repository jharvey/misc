### Things that could break the plan
- **Scholarship trade-off:** Finishing in 3 years means the Presidential Scholarship is used for 6 semesters, not 8. Year 4 is never paid for, so this is still a big saving (~$35k net), but confirm the scholarship terms with Financial Aid.
- **Parent PLUS lifetime cap ($65k per student):** This is fine for a 3-year plan. If the plan slips to 4 years, only ~$5k of PLUS is left for year 4.
- **Master's financing is different:** Parent PLUS does not cover graduate school, and Grad PLUS is gone for new borrowers after July 1, 2026. She can borrow up to **$20,500/yr** in her own unsubsidized loans (8.07% for 2026-27).
- **M.A. program is new (launched 2026):** Confirm licensure eligibility in the state she plans to work in (Maine vs. Massachusetts) and ask about accreditation plans.
- **Core transfer limit (~8 credits after matriculation):** Most summer community-college courses should count as electives or language. Don't plan on using them for Core without written approval.

## Money Plan

### Funding order (cheapest first)
1. **Scholarships/grants** (already in "net after aid")
2. **529**: no interest, so use it before borrowing, *but* leave **$4,000/yr** of tuition paid by loans or cash to claim the **American Opportunity Tax Credit (up to $2,500/yr)**, if our income qualifies
3. **Cash flow + work-study earnings**
4. **Her Direct Loans** (6.52% for 2026-27): ~$5.5k / $6.5k / $7.5k per year by grade level. *Confirm whether these are already counted in "net after aid."*
5. **Parent PLUS** (9.07% + 4.228% origination fee) last. Borrowing $16,250 nets only ~$15,560 after the fee.

### 529 rules to follow
- ✅ **Qualified:** tuition and required fees (Gordon, YCCC, SMCC, Gordon Plus), required books/supplies, a computer, and room & board **only when enrolled at least half-time**
- ❌ **Not qualified:** fuel/transportation, airfare/travel, passport, personal spending
- **THT 291:** ask Gordon for a cost breakdown. The tuition part is 529-eligible; the travel part generally is not.
- **Same calendar year:** take the 529 withdrawal in the same calendar year the bill is paid (a Spring bill paid in December → withdraw in December)
- **AOTC carve-out:** 529 withdrawal ≤ (qualified expenses − tax-free scholarships − $4,000)
- Keep every receipt and 1098-T, and keep Form 1099-Q with the tax file
- Keep the son's 529 for the son. Any leftover later can switch beneficiary (e.g., to the daughter's master's) or partly roll into a Roth IRA (subject to the rules)

### Work-Study / Campus Job
- **Reasonable target:** **8–10 hrs/week** during semesters (Gordon allows up to 20). That's ≈ **$3,000–4,000/yr** at ~$15/hr.
- With 15–18 credits + the Clinical track, staying ≤ 10–12 hrs protects GPA and the scholarship.
- Work-study is **need-based** and isn't guaranteed. You also have to find the job yourself. Regular campus jobs don't require a work-study award.
- Pay goes to **her as a paycheck**, not onto the bill. Earmark it for books, personal costs, and Summer 2027 travel.
- **Best fit:** psych research assistant, Counseling Center or academic support roles. These also strengthen the M.A. application.
- Work-study earnings are excluded from income on the next FAFSA.
